How this works

Pick a target (the record you keep) and a source (the duplicate you want to fold into it). All encounters, orders, admissions, vitals, nursing notes, and handovers from the source are appended onto the target. The source record is kept and marked as merged, so nothing is lost. Every merge can be undone from the history section below.
